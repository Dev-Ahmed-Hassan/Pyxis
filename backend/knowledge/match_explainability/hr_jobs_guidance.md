# HR Jobs Guidance

This document supports HR, recruiting, people operations, and non-engineering
technology-adjacent roles. It is useful when a student explores broader
business or coordination roles around technology companies.

## HR Intern Role Signals

chunk_type: career
field: hr
role: HR Intern
skills: communication, documentation, coordination, screening, employee records
purpose: role-fit

HR internships usually value communication, documentation, coordination,
attention to detail, confidentiality, scheduling, candidate screening, and
basic HR operations. Technical project evidence is less important than
organization, writing, and people-facing experience.

Strong evidence names:

```text
event coordination
candidate or participant communication
documentation
records management
confidentiality
reporting or spreadsheet work
```

## Technical Recruiter Role Signals

chunk_type: career
field: hr
role: Technical Recruiting Intern
skills: communication, sourcing, role understanding, screening, ATS
purpose: role-fit

Technical recruiting roles value communication and enough technical literacy
to understand job descriptions, skill requirements, and candidate profiles.
A CS student can be a good fit if they can explain technical skills clearly
and organize candidate information.

Evidence can include:

```text
technical writing
community coordination
project documentation
screening rubric
spreadsheet tracking
communication samples
```

## HR Analytics Project Suggestion

chunk_type: project
field: hr
role: HR Intern
skills: Excel, data analysis, dashboard, reporting
purpose: missing-skill-project

Use this project when the student wants HR or recruiting evidence from a CS or
IT background.

Project:

```text
Create a simple HR analytics dashboard using sample employee or recruitment
data. Track hiring funnel stages, time-to-hire, department counts, and basic
retention indicators.
```

Resume bullet:

```text
Built an HR analytics dashboard using sample recruitment data to summarize
hiring funnel stages, time-to-hire, and department-level trends.
```

## HR Interview Guidance

chunk_type: interview
field: hr
role: HR Intern
skills: communication, confidentiality, organization
purpose: interview-prep

Common HR interview questions:

```text
How do you handle confidential information?
Tell me about a time you coordinated people or tasks.
How would you communicate with a difficult candidate or employee?
How do you stay organized when tracking many applicants?
Why are you interested in HR despite a technical background?
```

Strong answers should show empathy, confidentiality, organization, and clear
communication.
